Operating instructions & house rules for your stay at Giglio Pad.

Address

Vicolo del Giglio, 2a
Roma, RM 00186

The closest major landmark is Piazza Farnese, which is the closest most cabs can reach.

Expenses

The condo is entirely free for you to use. But if you insist on covering expenses, it costs €70 to have the apartment cleaned, and utilities for the apartment cost roughly €10 a day.

Contact Dash if you would like to have the condo cleaned during your stay.

Household Items

Cabinet by the door: shopping bags, paper towels, shelf-stable foods.

Cabinet above the bathtub: toilet paper, sunscreen, soap, toothbrushes, first aid kit.

Living room cubbies: plugs, charging cables, fans, speaker, hats, towels, sheets, board games, office supplies.

Navigating the Condo

Front kitchen door: do not put your fingers in the door hinge. Don't leave the cabinet to the left of the refrigerator open.

Door from the living room to the bathroom: turn the handle completely when opening and closing the door.

Bedroom door: use light when going down the stairs (there are light switches both upstairs and downstairs).

Door from the veranda to the living room: this door can be hard to see when it’s open if the curtains are down. Careful not to walk into it.

Post Office

Do not trust online information (e.g. Google Maps) for post office locations and hours. The closest post office to the condo should be at Piazza Benedetto Cairoli, but it may be permanently closed. The one at Piazza San Silvestro is the closest post office that should be guaranteed to be open from at least 8 am until 12:30 pm, Monday through Saturday. Waits can be long, so arrive early.

Sending mail from the mail slot on the building and receiving mail at Vicolo del Giglio are unreliable.

Coffee

Barnum is the closest place to the condo to get American-style coffee and French pastries. You can skip the line of people outside by ordering at the bar to go.

Sant'Eustachio is best close option for high quality Roman coffee.

You won't always see it on menus, but you can order a "caffè freddo con latte" anywhere if you're looking for an American-style iced coffee (made with espresso).

Expressions

Informal / Formal:

  • How is it Going? – Come stai? / Come sta?
  • Thanks a million. – Grazie infinite
  • With pleasure – Con piacere
  • And to you. – A te / A lei
  • Excuse me – Scusi / Scusa
  • It's a pleasure to meet you – Piacere
  • Hello – Ciao / Salve / Salute / Buongiorno

Rental Cars

Take photos of the car from every angle (front, back, left, right, roof, and each of the four corners), whether or not you notice anything, and even if the agent at the pick up location notices issues and writes them down. As long as you have up-close photos of the car at the lot where you picked it up, you are unlikely to be fined for anything noticed upon returning the car (which there will be, even if the car looked new at pick up).

Transportation

Scooters and bikes: Set up your account and install the app and start the ride through the app. If you start a Lime ride directly through the QR code on the bike without having an account set up, it will be impossible to end the ride when you’re done and you’ll pay for an extra 15 €0.39 minutes.

Taxis & Uber: The closest taxi station is on the far side of Campo de' Fiori at Piazza di San Pantaleo. The next closest taxi stand is at Largo di Torre Argentina. Uber does exist but may take much longer than a cab; Uber may be cheaper than a cab, but that is not guaranteed.

Buses: You can buy paper bus tickets at all of the tabacchi stores (tobacco shops, signaled by a white 'T' against a black background) and at the hexagonal green tourist shop / newspaper stands you’ll find in most of the piazzas. Validate on the bus or pay by card.

Waste & Recycling

Nearest sorted trash bins: Located at Via della Pietà, 15.

Leave bagged trash around the corner on Vicolo delle Grotte next to the flamingo graffiti. Monday - Paper, Tuesday - Plastic, Wednesday - Mixed, Thursday - Paper, Friday - Plastic.

In the late afternoon, there are assorted bins at Campo, just in front of the Drunken Ship pub, for disposal of all types of waste including glass bottles and metal cans.

Laundry

In-Unit Washing Machine: The washing machine in the bathroom works, but is small and takes a long time. You can hang items on the veranda or from the clotheslines extending from it.

  • Once the washing machine is running, don’t touch anything. Don’t pause it. Don’t turn the dial. Let the entire cycle complete before trying to open the lid.
  • If the washing machine lid won’t open: Press the Reset button (the square button above the Avvio button) and wait for the process to finish before trying to open the door.
  • The “cotton” setting (60 degrees centigrade) is not recommended for anything other than towels and sheets.

Laundromat Across the Alley: The laundromat across the alley says that they open at 7 a.m., but they will be open by 9 a.m. You can pay by cash or card.

  • There is also a special card that you can buy to save 1 euro on each wash, but if the light on the card reader on the machine is red, then that special card won't work.
  • The washing machines do not require detergent.
  • The dryers can dry large loads in 30 minutes.

Air Conditioning

Turn on the A/C in the living room and turn on the fan in the sleeping nook to cool the sleeping nook down.

There are A/C units in the living room, kitchen, and master bedroom. All use the same remotes.

There is also a ceiling fan in the kitchen with its own remote.

Shower

Caution

The knob that controls the overhead shower will come off if pulled on at the right angle.

Patio

  • Don't play music or make loud noise on the patio.
  • Maximum occupancy on the patio is four people.
  • Keep the awning extended to the point where its edge is roughly above the railing.
